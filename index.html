I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Department of Mathematical, Physical and Computer Sciences of the University of Parma, working with Prof. Alessandro Dal Palù. My research spans computer graphics, geometry processing and deep learning, with applications to digital humans, physical simulation and environmental data.

My current project applies artificial intelligence to flood simulation, predicting the behaviour of water during floods in collaboration with the Department of Engineering and Architecture. In parallel, I lead a project on digital twins for package transportation, based on virtual physical simulation and developed with local companies.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Verona (2023), advised by Prof. Umberto Castellani, with a thesis on a modeling-by-matching approach for clothed human animation. Before joining Parma, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Verona and a research fellow at the University of Milan with Prof. Marco Tarini.

Digital Humans, Garments & AI

Deep learning for garment segmentation and cloth modeling, and animation-ready clothed avatars built from real-world 3D acquisitions.

Shape Correspondence

Functional maps, remeshing-by-matching, skeleton transfer and character retargeting between non-rigid 3D shapes.

Physics-Based Simulation & AI

Virtual crash tests and digital twins of palletized loads, with AI models trained on simulated data — from packaging stability to flood prediction.
